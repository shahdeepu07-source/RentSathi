❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I view my monthly bill?
Open the Bills tab from the sidebar or bottom menu. Your current bill is shown at the top, and older months are listed in the history table. Click View & Download Bill on any row to see the full breakdown, download it as an image or PDF, or print it.
How is my bill calculated?
Your bill is the sum of electricity units (previous reading subtracted from current reading), room rent, water charges and waste charges, plus any previous due amount. Any deduction applied by your owner is also shown separately.
How do I pay my bill?
Payments are arranged with your owner — the payment method is set on the bill by the owner (for example Cash, eSewa or Bank transfer). Online payment through the app is coming soon.
My bill shows a previous due — what does it mean?
A previous due means the previous month's bill was not fully paid. It gets added to your current month's total automatically. Contact your owner if you believe it is incorrect.
Why does my balance show an amount?
Your dashboard balance is the total amount outstanding for your room. It updates as bills are generated and payments are recorded. If something looks wrong, contact your owner first.
How do I change my password?
Click the Change password link at the bottom of the sidebar, enter your current password, then set a new one (at least 4 characters).
I have a problem the app doesn't solve. Who do I ask?
Fill in the support form above — include your contact details and a short description. The support team will reply to you as soon as possible.
