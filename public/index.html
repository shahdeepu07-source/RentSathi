❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I get started after registering?
After your first login you get a 30-day free trial. Open the Houses tab to add your house, then add tenants in the Tenants tab. You can start generating bills immediately — no payment needed during the trial.
How do I generate a bill for a tenant?
Go to the Billing tab, select the house and tenant, pick the month, enter the current meter reading and any deduction, then click Generate Bill. The tenant sees the bill instantly in their app and can download it as an image or PDF.
How is the bill amount calculated?
Total = electricity units × 15 (rate per unit) + room rent + water charge + waste charge + previous due, minus any deduction you apply. You can add a notice note that appears on the bill.
My trial ended. What happens now?
Once your trial ends you will not be able to log in until you get a subscription. Go to the login page and sign in to see the Subscription / upgrade area, choose Self-service or Full-service, pick monthly or yearly, and submit with your payment screenshot. The SuperAdmin approves it and your plan is activated.
How many tenants can I add?
Your plan has a tenant limit that is shown in your subscription area (the demo accounts allow up to 50). If you reach the limit you will be asked to upgrade to a larger plan.
How do I record a payment from a tenant?
Open a tenant's bill and click Record payment. Choose the payment type (full, partial or other), enter the amount if partial, and it is marked on the bill with a remark.
I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?
Deleted tenants and houses go to the Trash tab (SuperAdmin / Admin panel) where they can be restored. Items are only permanently gone when you use the permanent-delete button there.
How do I change my password or account details?
Use the Change password link at the bottom of the sidebar. Your name and contact details can be updated from your profile settings; for anything else, contact support.
