1. Introduction

Welcome to SajiloRent, a house and property billing platform provided by Gnomon Studio. This page explains our Privacy Policy (how we handle your data) and Terms of Service (the rules of using SajiloRent).

By creating an account or using SajiloRent in any form (web, Android app, or Windows app), you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. Information We Collect

Account data

  • Your name, username, email, phone number and address (when you register).
  • Your role (owner, tenant, or admin) and the houses you are linked to.

House & billing data

  • House details, tenant names and contacts, electricity readings, rent, and billing history that you enter into the platform.
  • Notice messages you create and send to tenants.

Technical data

  • Basic usage information such as your IP address, device type and browser, used only to keep the service secure and to detect misuse.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the SajiloRent service: billing, notices, house and tenant management.
  • To secure your account and protect the platform (for example, lockout after failed sign-in attempts).
  • To communicate with you about your account, billing or support requests.
  • To keep backups of your data to prevent loss.

We do not sell your personal information or your house data. We do not show third-party ads on your dashboards.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are stored as encrypted hashes. We never store plaintext passwords for regular accounts.
  • Data is stored on secure hosting providers with encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Failed sign-in attempts trigger temporary lockouts to stop unauthorised access.
  • You can back up and restore your own data from the platform.

5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. Owners solely control the data they enter about their own houses and tenants. Deletion of an account removes the account and, where requested, the associated data.

6. Accounts, Payment and Subscription

  • Owners sign up on a free trial, after which a subscription applies. Subscriptions are strictly per tenant or per plan as stated on the pricing page.
  • Payment during subscription periods is processed via trusted third-party payment partners (such as eSewa) and is subject to their terms.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials safe.

7. Acceptable Use

You agree not to use SajiloRent to:

  • Break any applicable law or regulation.
  • Impersonate other users or access accounts you are not authorised for.
  • Interfere with, damage or disrupt the service, its servers or other users' data.
  • Attempt to bypass any security, rate-limit or lockout mechanism.

8. Availability and Changes

We aim to keep SajiloRent available at all times, but the service is provided "as is" and "as available". We may update this policy and these terms from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.

9. Contact Us

For any questions about privacy or these terms, reach out to us and we'll support you quickly.
