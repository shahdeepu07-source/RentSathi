Features

Everything a house needs, nothing it doesn't

Core tools for day-to-day property management, built to be straightforward.

Manage tenants

Add, update and remove tenants per house, with contact details always at hand.

Manage owners

Keep property owners linked to their houses, with clear ownership records.

Bills & payments

Generate monthly bills, mark them paid, and keep a full history per tenant.

Notices

Send notices to tenants and owners so important updates reach the right people.

Role-based access

Admin, SuperAdmin, Owner and Tenant roles — everyone sees only what they should.

Web, Android & Windows
