❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?
Create an account on the login page — you get a 30-day free trial. Add your house, then add tenants, then generate your first bill. Everything works during the trial.
Is SajiloRent free?
New owners get a 30-day free trial with the full feature set. After the trial, choose Self-service or Full-service (monthly or yearly) and upgrade from your account — the SuperAdmin approves the plan.
How do tenants see their bills?
Each tenant gets a login. In the app they open the Bills tab to see the current bill, full breakdown, and previous months, and can download or print any bill.
What payment methods are accepted?
Upgrade payments are accepted by bank/eSewa transfer with a screenshot uploaded in the app. Online payment through the app is coming soon.
I deleted a tenant or house by mistake. Can I recover it?
Yes — deleted items go to the Trash in the Admin panel and can be restored anytime before they are permanently deleted.
Is my data safe?
Your data is stored securely on the server and is only visible to you, your tenants, and the support team when needed.
