# SajiloRent — Video narration scripts

Two files ship alongside the videos:

- `owner-guide.mp4` / `owner-guide.pdf` — for owners
- `tenant-guide.mp4` / `tenant-guide.pdf` — for tenants

Use these scripts to re-record your own voice-over (phone/OBS), or just send
the captioned video as-is. Each line matches one slide.

---

## Owner video (English)

1. **SajiloRent** — The simple way to manage your rental houses and bills.
2. **Log in** — Go to sajilorent.onrender.com, or open the app we gave you. Sign in with the username and password we provided.
3. **Your dashboard** — Here you see your houses, tenant counts and monthly totals at a glance. Pick a house to start.
4. **Generate a bill** — Open Create Bill, choose the tenant and enter this month's electricity reading. The app automatically calculates units used, electricity, rent, water and waste — don't worry about the math.
5. **Bill history** — Every bill you generate is saved in History. Reopen past months, mark bills as paid, or adjust a balance.
6. **Post a notice** — Need to tell tenants something? Write a notice. It appears on their app instantly.
7. **Need help?** — Use the Support tab in the app to message us anytime.

## Owner video (नेपाली)

1. **सजिलो रेन्ट** — घरभाडा र बिलको व्यवस्थापन गर्ने सजिलो तरिका।
2. **लगइन गर्नुहोस्** — sajilorent.onrender.com मा जानुहोस्, वा हामीले दिएको एप खोल्नुहोस्। कम्प्युटरको EXE वा मोबाइलको APK दुवैबाट सकिन्छ।
3. **तपाईंको ड्यासबोर्ड** — घरहरू, भाडा सङ्कलन र मासिक टोटल एकै ठाउँमा देखिन्छ। घर चयन गर्नुहोस्।
4. **बिल बनाउनुहोस्** — Create Bill मा जानुहोस्, भाँडालाई छनोट गरी यस महिनाको विद्युत रिडिङ हाल्नुहोस्। बिजुली, भाडा, पानी र फोहोर सबै आफैं जोडिन्छ।
5. **बिल इतिहास** — बनाइएका सबै बिल History मा सुरक्षित हुन्छ। पछिल्ला महिना हेर्नुहोस्, भुक्तानी मिलाउनुहोस् वा ब्यालेन्स समायोजन गर्नुहोस्।
6. **नोटिस पठाउनुहोस्** — भाडेरलाई भन्नुपर्ने छ भने नोटिस लेख्नुहोस्, उसको एपमा तुरुन्तै देखिन्छ।
7. **मद्दत चाहिए भने** — एपभित्रको Support बाट हामीलाई कुनै पनि बखत सन्देश पठाउन सक्नुहुन्छ।

## Tenant guide (English)

1. **SajiloRent** — Your monthly rent bill and owner updates, neatly in one app.
2. **Log in** — Open the app (or the website) and sign in with the tenant username and password your owner gave you.
3. **Your current bill** — This month's bill shows here as soon as your owner generates it — units, rent and the total amount.
4. **History** — All past bills below. See what you paid and what still needs to be paid, month by month.
5. **Owner notices** — Your owner's announcements show up front and center here.
